Wei Hu leads medical evaluation and research planning for Crystal. She draws on clinical and research experience to assess how the platform represents anatomy, pathology and clinical reasoning in medical education and research communication.

## Education and experience

- **2024** · Began postdoctoral research and received support through Fudan University’s Super Postdoctoral Program.
- **2023** · Earned a doctorate in internal medicine at Fudan University after entering its eight-year clinical medicine program in 2015.
- Studied in Karl Deisseroth’s lab at Stanford University and in Renwang Xue’s reproductive endocrinology lab during clinical training.

## Research

Her research uses multimodal imaging to study the neuroendocrine mechanisms of obesity, polycystic ovary syndrome and premature ovarian insufficiency, with an emphasis on individualized acupuncture interventions. She helped establish tissue-clearing 3D imaging and second near-infrared window (NIR-II) in vivo imaging platforms. Her work also uses optogenetics, single-cell sequencing and two-photon imaging.

## Funding and academic work

- Principal investigator for projects supported by the China Postdoctoral Science Foundation and the National Natural Science Foundation of China’s Young Scientists Fund.
- Contributed to an interdisciplinary project at the Shanghai Institute for Mathematics and Interdisciplinary Sciences and to Fudan University’s COVID-19 emergency research program.
- Publication record includes 23 SCI-indexed papers, including 11 as first author, joint first author or joint corresponding author. Patent record includes one granted invention patent and three applications.

## Honors

- Third prize at the National Undergraduate Forum on Basic Medical Innovation and Experimental Design.
- Distinguished Poster Award at the Shanghai Medical Colleges Undergraduate Innovation Practice Forum.
