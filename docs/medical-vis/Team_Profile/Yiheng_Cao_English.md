Yiheng Cao holds a PhD in medical information and image analysis. He began postdoctoral research at the Suzhou Institute of Biomedical Engineering and Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, in 2025. His work covers multimodal generative AI, dynamic medical image synthesis, and cross-modality registration. He has contributed to Crystal-related research on brain imaging, volume-rendering denoising, and multi-material visualization.

## Research projects

- **Multimodal cardiac image synthesis.** Investigates how to generate high-resolution dynamic cardiac images from ECG, chest radiographs, and routine clinical parameters to support more accessible cardiovascular screening research. Principal investigator on a 2025 National Natural Science Foundation of China Research Fund for International Young Scientists (RFIS-I) project.
- **Dynamic medical imaging for radiotherapy.** Researched respiratory motion synthesis from static CT during his PhD, exploring synthetic 4D CT for assessing lung tumor targets and motion in radiotherapy.
- **Dynamic bladder imaging.** Curated clinical imaging data, generated intermediate sequences from single or paired static CT scans, and delivered a prototype for preliminary evaluation by clinical collaborators.
- **Correlative light and electron microscopy navigation.** Contributes sequential within-modality registration, structural alignment across modalities, and algorithm benchmarking.
- **Frozen-section virtual staining and liver cancer recurrence.** Collaborates on H&E-style image translation and consistency evaluation. Also extracts quantitative features from follow-up CT for hepatocellular carcinoma recurrence modeling.
