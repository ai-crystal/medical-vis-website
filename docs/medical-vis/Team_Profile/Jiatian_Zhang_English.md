Jiatian Zhang researches AI for medical image processing and computer-aided diagnosis. His work focuses on brain disorders including meningioma, glioma and stroke, and applies deep learning, statistical machine learning, radiomics, self-supervised learning and visual foundation models.

## Education and honors

- **2026** · PhD in Engineering, Biomedical Engineering, University of Science and Technology of China (USTC).
- **2020** · Bachelor’s degree in Biomedical Engineering, Shandong University.
- Outstanding Graduate honors from Shandong University and USTC, university first- and second-class scholarships, and the ICBC Keling Student Scholarship.
- Seventh place nationally in the automatic glioma segmentation track of the 2021 Tencent Miying Medical AI Competition.

## Research projects

Contributed to research proposals for a Shanghai municipal science and technology initiative and the Fudan University Medical Engineering Fund. Took a lead role in preparing a 2023 research instrumentation and functional development proposal for the Chinese Academy of Sciences Magnetic Resonance Technology Alliance.
