Di Zhang contributes to the Crystal website’s design, full-stack development, and server operations. He has more than three years of experience digitizing laboratory workflows, with a focus on AI agents, data automation, and business process applications.

## Technical skills

### AI workflows and agents

- **Agent design and development.** Breaks business goals into stages that can be executed and verified independently, with clear inputs, outputs, and handoffs. Built the Litnebula workflow for e-book parsing, entity and relationship extraction, author enrichment, deduplication, and human review before publication.
- **Prompt engineering and structured outputs.** Uses prompts and JSON Schema to constrain model responses, with validation and retries for parsing failures. Routes uncertain results by confidence and human review, with batch records for traceability.
- **Retrieval, semantic matching, and tool integration.** Familiar with retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and the Model Context Protocol (MCP). Applied string matching, embedding similarity, and model-assisted review to resolve and deduplicate entities in Litnebula.
- **Workflow orchestration and quality control.** Uses background queues for batch imports, with concurrency limits, rate limiting, and cancellation. Covers critical paths with automated tests and records review decisions from extraction through publication.

### Full-stack engineering and business automation

- **Programming and web development.** Uses Python, SQL, and Linux for data tools and services, and FastAPI and SQLite for APIs and data storage. Experience with HTML/CSS, Vue 3, React, TypeScript, and Three.js, plus Nginx deployment and server operations.
- **Data processing and business intelligence.** Uses Python, SQL, and Power Query to clean, join, and standardize data, and Power BI to build dashboards for quality analysis, alerts, and operations.
- **Low-code and process automation.** Builds applications with Power Automate, Power Apps, and Mingdao Cloud HAP for data collection, approvals, notifications, reporting, and inventory tracking. Takes workflows from requirements through delivery and user training.
- **English and cross-functional collaboration.** Reads and writes technical documentation in English, communicates in writing, and presents project updates. Works with business and engineering teams to define delivery requirements.

## Education and experience

- **Shandong University of Technology**, bachelor’s degree in physics.
- **Lab Digital Specialist, consumer goods** (2022–2025). Built data processing and visualization tools for laboratories and automated workflows for sample registration, reporting, and inventory management.

- **Laboratory data management.** Built procurement and inventory workflows for requests, approvals, and stock tracking, with automated chemical name standardization.
